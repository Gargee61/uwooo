# 🤖 AI-AUTO — INTELLIGENT SALES & LEAD MANAGEMENT PLATFORM

> A smart, real-time platform designed to help businesses manage leads, analyze customer intent, track properties, automate site visits, and improve sales operations using AI-powered workflows.

---

## 🚀 ABOUT THE PROJECT

**AI-Auto** is an intelligent sales and customer-management platform developed during my software development internship at **Unified Web Options & Services (UWO)**.

The platform brings multiple sales activities into one centralized dashboard — from **lead intelligence and property inventory** to **site-visit scheduling and customer interaction tracking**.

The goal was to make the sales process more **data-driven, organized, and efficient** while providing a clean and responsive user experience.

---

## 🎯 PROJECT OBJECTIVE

The primary objective of AI-Auto is to simplify and automate the sales workflow by providing:

- 📊 Real-time lead monitoring
- 🧠 Intelligent lead analysis
- 🏠 Property inventory management
- 📅 Site visit scheduling
- 👥 Customer interaction tracking
- ⚡ Real-time data fetching
- 📈 Sales insights and analytics
- 🔔 Notifications and activity updates

---

## ✨ KEY FEATURES

### 📊 LEAD MANAGEMENT

- View total leads from a centralized dashboard
- Categorize leads into:
  - 🔥 Hot
  - 🟡 Warm
  - 🔵 Cold
- Monitor lead status and activity
- Track customer information
- Analyze lead-related data

---

### 🧠 LEAD INTELLIGENCE

AI-assisted lead analysis helps sales teams understand customer intent and prioritize high-value opportunities.

The system provides insights that help sales teams focus on leads with higher conversion potential.

---

### 🏠 PROPERTY INVENTORY MANAGEMENT

Manage property and project information from a centralized interface.

The system supports:

- 🏗️ Project name
- 📍 Location
- 💰 Price range
- 📝 Description
- 🏢 Property type
- 📌 Project status
- 🏠 Total units
- ✅ Available units
- 🚧 Construction progress
- 📅 Expected completion

---

### 📅 SITE VISIT AUTOMATION

The platform helps sales teams organize and track customer site visits.

Features include:

- Schedule site visits
- View upcoming visits
- Track visit status
- Connect visits with customer information
- Monitor scheduled activities

---

### 👥 CUSTOMER INTERACTION TRACKING

Keep customer interactions organized in one place.

The system helps sales teams track:

- Customer conversations
- Follow-ups
- Lead activity
- Interaction history
- Sales progress

---

### ⚡ REAL-TIME DATA

The dashboard is designed around real-time information so that important sales data can be accessed without constantly switching between different systems.

---

### 🔔 NOTIFICATIONS

A notification system keeps users informed about important activities such as:

- New leads
- Upcoming site visits
- Customer updates
- Sales activities
- System events
